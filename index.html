Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our interior woodwork services.

It starts with a free consultation! You can book online or call us. We'll discuss your requirements, measure your space, and provide initial design ideas and a rough estimate.
Most standard residential projects (like a modular kitchen or wardrobes) take 4–6 weeks from design approval to final installation. Larger, full-home interiors may take 8–12 weeks.
We use only premium, sustainably sourced materials, including BWP grade plywood, HDHMR boards, solid teak wood, and high-quality laminates/veneers. All hardware is from trusted brands like Hettich and Blum.
Our warranty covers all manufacturing defects, hardware malfunctions (like loose hinges or stuck channels), and material issues like unexpected delamination. It does not cover general wear and tear or water damage from plumbing leaks.
Initial design concepts are free. If you'd like detailed 3D renders before booking, we charge a nominal fee which is 100% adjusted against your final bill if you choose to proceed with us.
